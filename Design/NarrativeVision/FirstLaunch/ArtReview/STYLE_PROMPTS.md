# First-Launch Style Prompt Set

Date: 2026-07-10

Use case: `illustration-story`

Direction A below is retained only as rejected provenance. It produced realistic/painterly concepts and must not be reused for production.

The active production direction is defined in `StyleCandidates/DirectionB_MatchAligned/PRODUCTION_STYLE_LOCK.md`.

## Shared Style Core

Grounded stylized military graphic novel for a premium mobile tactical game. Realistic anatomy, scale, architecture, equipment, and materials translated into confident painterly shapes with restrained ink-like edge control. Cinematic natural light, rich local surface detail, readable silhouettes, practical dust and smoke, and a broad neutral/green/stone palette with limited warm emergency light. The fictional Middle Eastern city is modern, inhabited, and specific: concrete and stone apartments, market awnings, workshops, clinic activity, rooftop water tanks, road repairs, trees, bushes, utility poles, worn asphalt, dirt shoulders, and ordinary civic life. Serious and humane, never sensational.

Shared avoid list: no uniform orange desert filter, no generic sci-fi city, no stock foreign military, no real flags or insignia, no extremist/religious/political symbols, no readable generated writing, no logos, no subtitles, no HUD, no gore, no victim spectacle, no exaggerated superhero pose, no malformed hands, anatomy, weapons, vehicles, or architecture.

Composition: landscape 20:9 intent, with all essential faces/actions inside a centered 16:9 safe area and quiet upper-right space reserved for a future Skip control.

## `STYLE-01` Living Sahrin

Before sunrise at Old Market. Vendors raise metal shutters, a small clinic receives medicine crates, municipal road workers repair a worn lane, delivery vehicles maneuver through a narrow service street, apartment roofs carry water tanks, scattered trees and shop awnings catch cool dawn light, and one cargo aircraft crosses the pale sky. Show a city impatient to live, not a battlefield. Wide cinematic establishing shot, layered depth, human-scale detail, no dominant soldier.

## `STYLE-02` Crisis

Minutes after a coordinated attack near Old Market. Major Dalia Rahim, matching the supplied model and portrait references, directs a disciplined local JRC rescue beside a disabled convoy while responders pull survivors to cover. In the middle distance, a blocked civilian street and clinic route remain visible through dust and localized smoke. Urgency without graphic injury; Dalia is practical, focused, and restrained rather than heroic. Preserve the exact face, sunglasses, tan equipment family, body proportions, and compact rifle structure from the supplied references while removing real-world insignia.

## `STYLE-03` ARIA Boot

Interior of a damaged district coordination post just after blackout. A rugged Civic Relay terminal wakes from darkness, fractured cyan-white geometric signal fragments converging into one stable non-human ARIA identity: an abstract radial aperture and broken concentric command lattice, calm rather than menacing. The faceless Commander is present only as hands and a shoulder silhouette at the console. Dust in practical light, dead radios, partial map illumination, restrained amber emergency lamps. No readable text, no human face for ARIA, no holographic woman.

## `STYLE-04` Scale Stress Test

Same visual language applied to a later large combined-arms operation: local JRC infantry, armored vehicles, one helicopter, road logistics, civilian infrastructure, vegetation, and distant aircraft all remain readable in a wide tactical city-edge composition. Emphasize believable scale, grounded machinery, rich terrain, connected roads, and protected civic structures. This is a style-capability test, not first-launch canon. No UI markers or faction flags.
