# Fictional Gulf Style Reference

Date: 2026-05-05

This folder contains a custom WarlineCapture style candidate: a fictional Gulf-region coastal city under air/naval pressure, rendered as a premium 2.5D/3D isometric gameplay target.

The goal is to give WarlineCapture a more specific visual identity without copying other RTS games or depicting a real-world attack on a named country or city.

## Design Intent

- Fictional coastal Gulf metropolis.
- Warm stone, glass, solar canopies, seawalls, port roads, palms, arcades, civic domes, and dense modern city blocks.
- Air/naval threat pressure through aircraft silhouettes, missile trails, drones/boats, smoke, and fires.
- Friendly blue civil-defense command language versus enemy red assault language.
- Premium 2.5D/3D isometric tactical readability.

## Safety And IP Rules

- Do not reference Iran, Iraq, Dubai, UAE, Tehran, Baghdad, or any real location in image prompts.
- Do not use real flags, national emblems, real military insignia, real landmarks, or readable political text.
- Do not present the image as documentary, historical, or news-war art.
- Keep this as AI-generated visual direction unless it is rebuilt through an owned/licensed production art pipeline.
