## 4. Why the rifleman fails (what is known)
- **Mechanism.** A ground unit must fit inside its spawn pad's cells. Every cell outside the pad is treated as occupied (`SkirmishTypedSpawnConstraint.TryMask`). It must also pass `SkirmishSpawnPlacement.EnsureTraversable`, which checks walkability and surface admission against the map's `MapSurfaceComponent` movement mask.
- **What the bitmap can't show.** The layout-dump bitmap at both pad positions shows clear cells (flag 0), but it carries no surface movement mask. The likely cause is native walkability or surface admission on these cells, unproven.
- **The probe that couldn't run.** An area measurement of a throwaway probe draft (606 areas covering the map, `codex\notes\w29-probe`) fails before it measures. That measurement needs the host session to start (`win-dev-unity\w29-probe-S074`, exit 2, same rifleman BlockedSpawn).
- **No data-only way left.** I see no further data-only diagnostic. The S002-only allocator capture (`S002StartupAllocatorCapture.cs:55`) would show the requested cell and its walkable, blocked and occupied state.

## 6. Surface diagnosis and fix (lead step `d1d4bad2d`: offline read-only, at most 2 placement attempts)
- **Surface decode.** `codex\notes\w29_surface.py` decodes `Assets/Game/Data/MapSurfaces/Match_Map_MapSurfaceData.asset`, read-only: gzip payload v3, single-layer, 2048×1024, 12-byte samples (`MapSurfaceDataAsset.TryReadSingleLayerGridPayload`). It applies the placer's rule, `MapSurfaceTraversalValidation.CanTraverse`:
  - infantry: mask bit 0, and slope ≤ 35° or road-like;
  - vehicles: mask bits 1|2, and slope ≤ 18° or road-like;
  - never: Blocked type or the Reserved flag.
  - Result for the playable window: 60,000 cells, 51,112 infantry-admitted, 46,513 vehicle-admitted.
- **Cause, proven offline.** A north-west strip (about x 740–808, z 552–660) is admitted for neither infantry nor vehicles, but the layout-dump bitmap shows it as clear (flag 0).
  - The run-c infantry pad (816,575.5) had **13/120** infantry-admitted cells: one rifleman fit, the second failed.
  - The run-d pad (800,600, the S073 deployment) had **0/120**.
- **Rounding check.** The layout frame is rotated ~3.6° from the map axes. Each new pad was therefore checked with a 1 m margin on every side: **all cells admitted**. Centres are on .5 m in x, so the pad edges do not fall on exact cell edges. This is the W24 lesson.
