# M2–M4 guard and movement audio review — 2026-09-10

## Findings and changes

- The accepted M2 design is Build plus Produce, with no combat encounter (`Design/Architecture/m02_establish_base_implementation_tracker.md`, acceptance at line 267 and owner decision dated 2026-08-29). Its shared-map towers lacked the dormancy protection already used by M3 and M4. `CampaignMissionSpawnSystem.MapDefenses` now applies that existing policy to peaceful M2, including parked map vehicles. Mission actors and produced units remain active; leaving the mission restores only entities disabled by this policy. An explicit M2 Defend objective retains the existing opt-in combat behavior.
- The real M2 baseline did **not** reproduce tower shots or weapon-fire requests. It did reproduce repeated `Gameplay.Unit.Engine.Vehicle.Move` events from twelve soldier entities: eight background actors and the four playable squad members. Multiple audio channels played `game_unit_engine_vehicle_move_01` concurrently.
- `UnitGridAuthoring` bakes `UnitVehicleMovement` tuning onto infantry as well as vehicles. `UnitMotionAudioSystem` incorrectly treated the presence of that shared data as proof of vehicle identity. It now uses `UnitMovementBehavior.UsesVehicleMotion`, matching the movement owner. Infantry and unclassified tuning-only entities cannot emit engine audio. Real vehicle and aircraft audio remains available.
- Tower weapon audio already originates from an eligible damaging shot in `BuildingDefenseAttackSystem`. No separate looping shooting source was found on the guard-tower prefabs. That behavior is preserved and covered with additional regression cases.

## Editor validation

All runs used `Tools/CI/invoke_unity_macos.sh` with GUI licensing and explicit timeouts. No Android validation was performed.

The focused NUnit run passed **75/75** tests: building defenses 30, motion audio 9, M2 launch 18, M2 wave rules 9, M3 map isolation 1, and M4 integration 8. The new cases verify no audio for absent, allied, neutral, dead, airborne, debug, or out-of-range targets; no detached shot during cooldown or after target removal; silent dormant map defenses; reversible mission isolation; and normal player-built tower and vehicle audio.

The architecture NUnit run passed **141/141**, including source-growth and ECS ownership guardrails, with no skipped or failed tests. Both test wrappers exited with code 0.

`MissionTowerFireEditorProbe` launches each real mission from the menu with an isolated temporary save. It observes the introduction and 40 gameplay seconds after the opening, records actual shot counters, audio requests and playing clips, and orders all four M2 squad members toward the base through the normal move-order path.
