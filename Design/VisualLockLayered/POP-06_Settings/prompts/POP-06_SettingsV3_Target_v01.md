# POP-06 Settings V3 Target v01 Prompt

Use case: `ui-mockup`

Create a final V3 target-lock reference for the Warline Capture mobile landscape settings popup. Preserve the approved sharp, colorful military command UI and the dimmed Match HUD backdrop from the pause flow.

Use a large centered modal with a full-width `COMMAND SETTINGS` header, gear icon, and square `X` close button. Under the header, place a strong left-aligned vertical tab rail with four large sharp rectangular buttons: `AUDIO` selected, then `GAMEPLAY`, `VIDEO`, and `ACCESSIBILITY`. Show only the selected Audio content in the main panel.

The Audio panel must show large mobile controls for `MASTER VOLUME 80%`, `MUSIC VOLUME 60%`, `SOUND VOLUME 85%`, and toggles for `MUSIC ON`, `SOUND ON`, and `VOICE ON`. Use thick bright slider tracks, large handles, and explicit rectangular ON states.

Attach the footer directly to the bottom of the modal. Place `RESET` on the left and a larger bright-green `APPLY` button on the right.

Use sharp rectangular corners, straight hard edges, solid charcoal panels, white condensed military typography, cyan and blue selected states, green enabled states, amber reset accents, thin metal rails, and crisp drop shadows. Do not use rounded or chamfered corners, gold ornamental borders, old thin sci-fi tracing, tiny controls, top tabs, a four-section grid, repeated controls, fake currencies, or water and naval imagery.
