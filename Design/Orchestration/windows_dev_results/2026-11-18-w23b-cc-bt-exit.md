## 1. Code read: why ARIA could not order a move to the W23 exit (read-only, no code changed)
The hypothesis from W23 holds. ARIA cannot tap a point at the playable edge.
- **Where ARIA taps.** The tap point is the objective area's centroid (`SkirmishNativePublicGoalPresentation.cs:120-136`).
- **When a tap point counts as available.** `SkirmishMatchView.PublicObjectiveHud.cs:211-228` projects it to the screen and requires it to be inside the screen safe area. Then `MatchHudAssistantUiSystemHelper.Watch.cs:290-300` clears it if `EventSystem.RaycastAll` hits any UI graphic at that point. A point under the right-hand HUD therefore becomes unavailable.
- **The camera cannot bring an edge point to the middle of the screen.** The camera clamp keeps the whole ground footprint of the view inside `CameraMin/CameraMax` (`RtsCameraSystem.SmoothTransitions.cs:34`, `76-87`; `RtsCameraSystem.cs:672-709`). An exit 4 m from the edge stays near the screen's right edge, under the HUD.
- **How that becomes a stop.** `StepPublicObjective` (`AriaSkirmishPlanSystem.Objective.cs`) has a 30 s deadline (line 31). It returns early while `!target.Available` (lines 89-93), so the ground tap (Stage 3, `order_tapped`) never happens. It does not refocus the camera while `GoalInView` is true (line 73). The result is `order_retry` every ~30 s. After `MaxOrderFailures = 6` (`AriaObjectiveSkillPolicy.cs:44-45`) it logs `order_blocked`, and the planner stops with `[AriaBlocked] reason=101` (`AriaSkirmishPlanSystem.cs:59`), or 111 after 180 s with no objective progress (`AriaPlayDecisionSystem.cs:37`).
- **Margin needed (inferred from the clamp and the default framing, not measured).** About 40–44 m from the edge at camera height 34, 48 m at height 40, 56 m at height 48. About 60 m is a safe allowance.
