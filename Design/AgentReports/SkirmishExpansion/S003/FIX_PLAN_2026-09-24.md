## Architecture decisions

- Keep scenario differences in typed definitions, roster/setup compilation and policy data. Do not introduce an S003 controller or scenario-ID branches inside shared gameplay systems.
- Shared building, unit, navigation, combat, production, resource and aircraft systems own execution. Expansion services validate scenario eligibility and submit typed requests; views only present state and forward input.
- Use one authoritative resource ledger per faction. Expansion stocks must become projections or be removed with a versioned migration; do not synchronize two mutable banks every frame.
- Apply role overlays through the shared stat/domain policy, with one damage owner. No additional per-frame structure damage path for production units.
- Starting grants, paid queues, refunds, destruction and restore each have explicit, idempotent ownership. Objective identity follows the original spawned Barracks, not any later replacement.
- The compiled setup drives briefing, match rules, HUD, enemy policy and ARIA observations. Never infer gameplay from display text.
