## Approval Scope

The user reviews only identity and reusable visual language:

- `Approve Dalia` or `Reject Dalia`
- `Approve Samira` or `Reject Samira`

Approval locks each character's face or core identity, body/proportion language, clothing/equipment family, palette, and low-poly rendering treatment. Expressions, poses, damage, and lighting may vary later while preserving the approved identity.
