portrait assets were preserved; authored comic crops support both aspect ratios.

The Persian intro Samira capture caught early typewriter reveal. It is not used
as full-caption fit evidence. The completed Persian Samira/ARIA debrief captures
were reviewed; this review does not claim every frame or every caption capture
was visually accepted. Native visual review is distinct from the passing full
normal-input automated journeys in `../readiness.md`.

validation and physical-device performance remain separate and unperformed gates.
