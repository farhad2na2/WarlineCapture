# WarlineCapture main menu visual contract

Updated 2026-09-28. Approved direction: [Campaign-led home/header v01](AgentReports/MenuHeaderMonetization/Mockups/home-campaign-header-v01.png) and its [review record](AgentReports/MenuHeaderMonetization/Mockups/home-campaign-header-v01.md).

## Composition

- Slim full-width dark header: native WARLINE CAPTURE logo, actual earned Credits, Settings. Credits are informational. No Command, energy or currency purchase plus-buttons.
- Main area approximately three quarters of the safe width: current Campaign intro art, Campaign label, chapter/mission, mission title, concise purpose and a large green Continue Campaign control. Copy, art and destination consume the same progression target. Missing art uses a neutral background.
- Two substantial independent mode cards below: green Operations and orange-red Skirmish, with mode purpose and native art/icons.
- Right column: compact cyan ARIA, selected Commander portrait/name and View Commander; smaller stacked Store and Armory actions below.
- The user approved [full-panel scene v02](AgentReports/MenuHeaderMonetization/Mockups/home-full-panels-v02.md) on 2026-09-29. ARIA uses a command-room background beneath her exact original portrait; all six saved commander choices use their corresponding full headquarters comic scenes. Store uses a collection emblem; Armory uses a shield and crossed weapons.
- ARIA retains `Assets/Game/Art/UI/V3Shared/Portraits/ARIA_MainMenu_V3.png` unchanged. Preserve aspect ratio; the generated mockup face is rejected.
- Retain Oxanium typography, native portrait choices, colorful controls and mobile spacing. Render labels and controls natively. The approved mockup is never a flattened shipping background.

## Navigation and onboarding

Fresh profiles follow cold open → ARIA → identity → M01 → debrief → headquarters, under [first-player flow](First_Player_Experience_And_Story_Onboarding_Design.md). After M01, Continue targets M02. Preserve authored disclosure: secondary modes/store become visible at their milestone; Settings and Commander stay available. Story Archive remains reachable through Campaign.

Continue opens Campaign selection focused on its presented mission for review; never deploys, starts ARIA or skips story. Revalidate activation. Completion offers Choose Mission; restrictions explain progression, ownership or readiness without implying purchase repairs unavailable content. Operations, Skirmish, Commander, Settings, Store and Armory retain functional routes and normal back/focus behavior.

## Campaign completion home

Follow the approved [completion-home handoff](AgentReports/CampaignCompletionHome/Handoff.md). Aggregate first-clear completion must cover the required registered catalog and its accessible runtime bindings; unlocked subsets, missing plates and an empty next mission do not establish completion. Pending resume retains its mission action.

Today's end of available content shows All Available Missions Completed, hides the chapter/mission row, and pairs one of the three generic aftermath scenes with its native localized caption. A scene/caption pair remains stable during a home visit, balance refreshes, locale changes and overlays. A new visit rotates without immediate repetition. Campaign Complete and the canonical epilogue require the entire authored ending to be registered, ready and cleared.

Choose Mission opens selection without deployment. The completed-home Story Archive chooser lists earned mission sequences and fully cleared chapter conclusions only, rechecks progress before playback, and uses the existing narrative canvas with a close/return path. It does not grant rewards or expose unplayed future previews.

## Responsive and accessible layout

Use 1920×1080 and wide landscape references with safe areas. Hero and mode cards absorb extra horizontal width, while the right column remains anchored. Controls are at least 80 reference pixels tall where applicable. Support Persian, long labels, enlarged text, valid zero/large balances and keyboard focus. Hidden controls must neither raycast nor receive focus.

## Acceptance

Compare implemented native EN/FA home at both ratios against approved v01. Verify actual routes, Campaign clear/reload/completion/missing-art cases, saved identity, unchanged ARIA, account settlement refresh and all required normal-input mission/result/return journeys. Record mockup approval, native review, automation, agent play, human/device acceptance and billing/content release gates separately. Compilation or fixture captures do not establish player readiness.

The old commander-dominated layout, three mode stack, Command strip, full-width shopping footer and obsolete deployment hotspots are superseded.
