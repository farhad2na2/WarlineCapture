## Non-Negotiable Visual Contract

- Preserve visibly faceted low-poly geometry on terrain, buildings, tents, props, vehicles, vegetation, and characters.
- Preserve simplified, slightly blocky POLYGON-style proportions.
- Use flat color-block materials with minimal texture detail; no photoreal surfaces, skin, foliage, cloth, dirt, or concrete.
- Use the Match palette: pale sand, tan canvas, olive and charcoal containers, muted green props, gray concrete barriers, dark asphalt, and sparse low-poly vegetation.
- The rural town grows from the Military-pack vocabulary: tents, container buildings, low concrete structures, guard towers, barriers, water tanks, crates, workshops, road checkpoints, and utility props.
- Use an elevated three-quarter/isometric cinematic camera compatible with Match gameplay, not a ground-level photographic lens.
- Enrichment comes from set density, connected roads, ground variation, lighting, shadows, atmosphere, and human activity, not realistic rendering.
- No real flags, insignia, generated writing, logos, readable signs, religious landmarks, current-conflict references, gore, or UI.

## Story Beat

`FL-P01`, Sahrin before the attack: a functioning rural town at the edge of a military response base. Civilians open small market stalls, a clinic receives supply crates, municipal workers repair a road shoulder, delivery vehicles move through the connected lane, and an aircraft crosses the dawn sky. The scene must feel inhabited, orderly, and hopeful before the crisis.
