## 5. Fix 3 diagnostic: why the convoy repair never starts
- **Proof totals:** 11 service Move choices. Zero accepted `Hold` and zero `Repair` orders across all CE runs.
- **Pattern:**
  - The truck is sent toward its service pad. After 70–110 s the journey asks for Hold.
  - The Hold is never accepted, and the stage hands back about 22 s later.
  - Example: S048 @130365, group 25. Service Move at 75.9 s, Hold choice at 168.5 s, back to the convoy at 190 s, Move accepted 203.8 s.
- **`[VehicleRepairQuote]` (21 lines):**
  - `reason=distance=315–347` appears when a damaged truck is selected near its start.
  - `reason=moving` appears when a truck is inside the 12 m service radius but still moving.
  - No `threat`, `facility`, `materials` or `rules` refusal was seen.
- **Conclusion:** the truck is never stopped at the pad, so `TryQuote` refuses with `moving`. The service detour costs 100–130 s per truck and repairs nothing. In S048 @130365 that alone covers the missed second delivery.
- **Not fixed here**, per the lead's "don't change repair rules". The next step is on the journey side: an accepted Hold, or a stop inside the pad radius before the Repair stage.
