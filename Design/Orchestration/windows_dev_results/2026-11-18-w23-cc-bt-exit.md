## 5. Why ARIA stops (diagnosis, not fixed)
- Each abort follows `[AriaObjective] event=order_blocked failures=6`: the objective Move order to the exit was retried six times (`MaxOrderFailures`) and then the planner set Blocked (`[AriaBlocked] reason=101`, once 111 = 180 s with no objective progress; intent `EvacuateOriginals`).
- Order counts in the menu logs: W9 CC BT 25 accepted, 0 retried or blocked; W23 CC BT 9 accepted, 35 retried or blocked. The same pattern appears in W23 Mountain Pass (see those files).
- Hypothesis, not proven: the new exit is at the east edge of the playable area (x up to 1296 of 1300). If the camera cannot centre past the map edge, the exit may sit under the right-hand HUD (the play area ends at screen x 1444 of 1920) where ARIA cannot tap it. Confirming this needs a camera/tap capture or an ARIA code read, which is shared code.
- A data-only alternative: move the exit inward so its centre is well inside the camera range, then re-measure and rerun. Not done here.
