## Acceptance and execution order

1. **Movement and animation.** Reproduce moving infantry/specialist animation at tactical and close camera distances. Verify actual movement, selected GPU clip, visible renderer clip and changing rendered pose. Fix stationary fallback while moving and any actor/LOD propagation issue found. Exercise move, chase, fire, stop, boarding, disembarkation and death.
2. **Camera and action readability.** Check both opening tours against the actual subjects, their RTS return, active warning jumps, user pan/follow behavior, and endings. Frame live actors/action instead of fixed empty-ground coordinates. Preserve player control outside intentional camera sequences and verify skip/reduced-motion/teardown.
3. **Mission completeness.** Repair Campaign objectives/star labels, ensure guidance and all class entries remain bilingual, verify reward/result/next-mission flow, and tune the M4 pursuit/rescue timing and M3 idle pressure against repeatable play strategies. Complete supported voice/audio scope without silently removing a requirement.
4. **Architecture and regression failures.** Work through every failure in the nine-fixture, 137-test architecture audit and the broader mission/legacy regression runners. Fix source ownership, dependencies, lifecycle and allocation problems. Correct a stale test only when its replacement asserts the intended contract and document why; do not suppress failures, relax budgets, or add blanket exceptions.
5. **Integrated Editor QA.** Run M3 and M4 success, failure, retry/replay, guidance and camera journeys against the final generated assets, both languages and aspect ratios. Include real movement/rendered animation, actual screen visibility, saved rewards, clean actor teardown, and timing/allocation measurements. Record exact run markers and source hashes. Human enjoyment is not a unit-test result; document concrete play observations and remaining limitations honestly.
6. **Delivery.** Reconcile acceptance checklists, commit and push the verified code/assets and text reports. Keep evidence images and recordings local.
