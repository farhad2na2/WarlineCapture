# First-Launch Opening Reference V2

Date: 2026-07-10

Status: Gate 5 revision draft

## Purpose

This is a reference-only opening animatic contract. Retail playback is assembled by the future layered runtime narrative player; no MP4, dialogue card, portrait, icon, menu, or choice state is baked into retail playback.

## Clarity Contract

- `0-17.5s`: name Sahrin and Old Market, show normal life, and let audible city ambience establish the place.
- `17.5-34.5s`: show separate attack, power, road, relay, and command failures.
- `34.5-58.5s`: introduce Major Dalia Rahim and Engineer Samira Haddad by full name, role, voice, portrait, and action.
- `58.5-88.5s`: introduce ARIA by name and function, explain damaged authority, and identify Old Market as the immediate priority.
- Commander identity and guidance then open as the separate live UI references in `ArtReview/PresentationCandidates/RevisionB_UserFeedback/interactive_ui/`. Their player-controlled duration is excluded from this linear reference video.
- `88.5-138.5s`: confirm player authority, explain the coordinated pattern, establish surviving JRC forces and civilian stakes, and issue a bounded lawful command.
- `138.5-176.5s`: identify the armed Ash Line patrol, separate hostiles from civilians, hand over tactical control, and state the first three game actions.

## Presentation Contract

- Use the approved off-white graphic-novel 9-slice direction from `DIALOGUE-B_GraphicNovel_APPROVED_REFERENCE.png`.
- ARIA uses the production focus-reticle icon; Dalia and Samira use approved portraits.
- Dialogue uses TMP and character-by-character reveal in runtime. The reference video may approximate that reveal but does not define the implementation.
- Identity, guidance, Skip, and every choice are live UI and are absent from the cinematic frame sequence.
- The opening uses clearly audible Old Market ambience, one aircraft flyby, then localized attack/blackout and radio cues. Music must not erase the normal city before the disruption.

## Review Boundary

The opening ends at the illustrated M01 handoff. M01 gameplay, debrief, and command-base reveal are reviewed separately; they are not connected by a fake gameplay placeholder inside this video.
