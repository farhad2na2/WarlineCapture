  - **W23b:** 4 originals were lost by 139.4 s while the exit was still closed, and the 5th loss ended the match at 141.2 s.
  - The log does not record where they died.
- **Likely cause (unproven).** The new exit sits in front of the defense line, 51–55 m from the fuel bladder and tower b. The trimmed routes no longer pass through a corridor. The originals probably wait at or move toward the exit inside the defenses' reach before any corridor is cleared. Before W23, the corridors lay between the start and the exit.
