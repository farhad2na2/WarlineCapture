## 1. Diagnosis
**How 115 happens:** `AriaPlayDecisionSystem` stops ARIA with reason 115 when a planner step presents the same control for a fourth consecutive press. `touch.Attempts` resets only when the target or goal changes.

**S038, CC BT, Mac lane 45 `7de2a3c79`, 130365.** `[AriaBlocked] reason=115 kind=Control intent=EvacuateOriginals`, `control=Squad0 selectedGroups=0`, at 225 s with 6/8 evacuated. Lane 45 log `:630729` / `:630745`; the lead supplied the excerpt.
- **Not boarding:** there are no PublicBoarding lines in range.
- **Presser:** the escort turn sets intent Advance, so the step pressing Squad0 was the objective choice order (`StepObjectiveOrder`, intent EvacuateOriginals). Its select stage presses the group card through `Publish`.
- **The W27 cause 1 bound** (`RejectedAttempts`, `MaxRejectedObjectiveContacts = 2`) lives in `AriaObjectiveTouchPlan`. That plan is rebuilt whenever the roster revision, presentation epoch or navigation group changes.
- **What reset it:** evacuations change the roster. Completed went 2/8 → 4/8 at 216.7–216.9 s, just before the stop. That reset the planner's count while the input layer kept counting presses of the same card, so the fourth press was refused with 115.
- **Not W27 cause 1 again (off page):** `GroupTarget` returns the group's own card.

**S010, DB FC, W16 proof `edb61d5d9`, 130365.** `[AriaBlocked] reason=115 control=Move intent=Advance supportStage=2 selectedGroups=0`, at 233.9 s (`w16-proof\menu\unity.log:23700`).
- `AriaSupportOrderPolicy` stage 2 presses Move to arm the selected support unit. Stage 2 never re-checks the selection before arming.
- The unit's selection was lost, so Move with nothing selected never armed. The input layer stopped ARIA on the fourth press, before the 12 s order deadline could release the order.
