# CH02 M05 Route Reopened — Canonical Mission Direction

Status: Visual-direction review pending before runtime implementation.

## Canonical authority

- Mission ID: `saga.ch02.m05.route_reopened`
- Chapter: Chapter 2 — Broken Grid
- Operation fantasy: Keep relief and Fuel moving while breaching the Ash Line logistics hub that controls the district's stolen routes.
- Primary goal: Sustain the recovery network, capture the hub, and recover its routing records.
- Required consequence: Protocol Fragment 2 proves that diverted resources were feeding selected dormant Civic Relay nodes.
- Chapter handoff: An encrypted signal escapes into a populated district and opens Chapter 3's network hunt.

## Player-facing mission structure

1. Split the starting force between the active relief network and the assault route.
2. Keep two authored lifeline lanes operating.
3. Restore one disrupted road link under pressure.
4. Breach and capture the logistics hub without destroying the systems or routing archive.
5. Hold the records long enough for ARIA to correlate Fuel, power, road, and market diversions.
6. Complete the Protocol Fragment 2 sequence and return to Campaign.

## Narrative and voices

- Samira Haddad: Owns civilian-service priorities and identifies machinery and records that must survive.
- Major Dalia Rahim: Owns force split, defensive coverage, and controlled breach priorities.
- ARIA: Identifies the routing archive, correlates live deliveries with dormant Relay activity, and assembles Protocol Fragment 2.
- Nadir Qassem: Frames the city's emergency traffic as authorization for his Relay activation strategy.

English and conversational Farsi captions and authored local voice clips are required. Runtime network TTS is prohibited.
