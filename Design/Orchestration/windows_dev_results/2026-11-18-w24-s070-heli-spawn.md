## 1. Cause
The trigger was the **player's** satellite dish and approach watchtower, not anything near the enemy pad.
- **The difference.** In the S070/S072 drafts these two structures come from S052, at (904,462) and (919.5,448). S064/S066 have them at (1092,387.5) and (1067,407.5).
- **Why it matters.** The authored layout frame is sized by the outermost anchors. The S052 positions widen it: `worldWidthMetres` goes from 372.99 to 419.42 and `originX` from 963.93 to 917.49 (`SkirmishLayout_S064` vs `SkirmishLayout_S070`).
- **What changes in float32.** Every structure goes through that frame (`SkirmishMapLayoutConfig.TryProjectLocalToMap` / `TryProjectToMap`). Draft positions that sit exactly on a cell edge then round the other way. The enemy helipad's west edge is x 1253.0 on a grid that starts at x 860, so its cell is `preferred=(392,160)` in S070 against `(393,160)` in S064. The fuel bladder and watchtower shift by one cell the same way.
- **The failed spawn.** With the helipad at 392, `[SkirmishStartingForce] failed prefab=Unit_Veh_Helicopter_Transport faction=2 member=0` follows, then `expandedStartupFailure=BlockedSpawn`.
- **Ruled out:**
  - The enemy air pad and the helicopter's requested cell are the same in both layouts. Projected in float32, the pad centre is (1261.0, 467.5), the requested cell is (401,167), and the 208 allowed cells are identical (`SkirmishTypedSpawnConstraint.TryMask` / `TryRequestedWorld`).
  - The rosters match. The faction-2 TransportHeli is in both S064 and S070.
  - The convoy pads, the destination and the moved enemy dish and towers are all 40 m or more from the pad. The W23/W23b destination moves did not change the failure either.
  - `TryTagActualOpeningMember` applies only to faction-1 originals.
- **Why there is no recovery.** Air units take only their exact requested cell (`SpawnCellSearchKernel.TryFindInitial`, lines 30–32). The fallback search uses the ground rule, which rejects blocked cells such as the helipad building. A conflict at the requested cell therefore ends the opening (`SkirmishScenarioSpawnSystem.cs:346-354`). The exact conflicting cell was not captured: the allocator capture is S002-only (`S002StartupAllocatorCapture.cs:55`), and widening it is shared code.
