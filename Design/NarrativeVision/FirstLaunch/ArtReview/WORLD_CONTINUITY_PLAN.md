## Old Market Geography Contract

- One connected dark asphalt road crosses the center third and remains the dominant playable route.
- A narrow service lane and road excavation create a believable flank/repair route without breaking connectivity.
- The civilian edge contains a small market, a clinic built from a restrained shop shell, workshops, roof water tanks, utility poles, and contemporary infrastructure.
- An open intersection provides the M01 contact zone; low barriers, carts, roadside debris, and curbs provide light cover without blocking the route.
- Sparse grass, bushes, stones, dirt, worn paving, and planted trees prevent a flat one-color ground treatment.
- Distant military response/logistics infrastructure may appear, but it is not the player's headquarters or the M02 abandoned forward post.
- No readable signs, generated regional writing, flags, real insignia, religious landmark, or baked UI appears.

## Persistent State Contract

| State | Allowed changes | Invariants |
|---|---|---|
| Living morning | Market opens, clinic delivery, road work, ordinary traffic, aircraft cue | Geography, camera family, sun, road, clinic, and landmarks |
| Attack/blackout | Localized blasts, failed practical lights, one blocked road end, smoke/dust, emergency response | Same geography; blackout occurs at dawn, not at night |
| M01 handoff | Readable player/move/patrol spacing and light cover | Same damage and route; approved frame becomes future 3D authority |
| Debrief | Route reopened, smoke reduced, responders/civilians advance | Damage persists; no unexplained reset |

## Relay And Command Contract

- The damaged command post is an improvised local coordination room, not a science-fiction bridge.
- Use radios, rugged electronics, briefing surfaces, cables, dust, cracked screens, generator power, cyan ARIA light, and restrained amber warning light.
- ARIA's approved identity is not the Civic Relay emblem. Relay infrastructure needs a separate neutral physical/network vocabulary.
- The post stabilized in `FL-P22` is the district coordination post and first command-base entry point. It is not the abandoned forward post restored in M02.
- Screens remain abstract and text-free; runtime UI supplies all names, choices, objectives, and status.
