# WarlineCapture Marketing Asset Workflow

This folder holds marketing-video samples and validation artifacts generated from the current WarlineCapture design docs, economy rules, monetization strategy, and visual-lock targets.

## Current Sample

- `SampleVideo/WarlineCapture_Sample_Marketing_Video.mp4` - 20 second 1920x1080 marketing-video sample assembled from approved design images.
- `SampleVideo/WarlineCapture_Sample_Marketing_Video_Preview.png` - contact sheet for fast visual review.
- `SampleVideo/WarlineCapture_Sample_Marketing_Video_Manifest.json` - source docs, source images, scene copy, duration, and verification data.
- `SampleVideo/WarlineCapture_Sample_Marketing_Video_QA.md` - human-readable pass/fail QA checklist.

## Generative Cinematic Concepts

- `GenerativeVideoConcepts/README.md` - workflow for AI-generated concept-cinematic marketing clips that do not use UI screenshots or gameplay target screens as footage.
- `GenerativeVideoConcepts/WarlineCapture_Generative_Cinematic_Brief.md` - creative brief, required beats, constraints, and approval criteria.
- `GenerativeVideoConcepts/WarlineCapture_Generative_Cinematic_Shots.json` - API-ready prompts for five 8 second concept clips.
- `GenerativeVideoConcepts/WarlineCapture_Generative_Cinematic_QA.md` - automated and human review checklist.
- `GenerativeVideoConcepts/Outputs/WarlineCapture_Generative_Cinematic_Storyboard.png` - dry-run storyboard contact sheet.
- `GenerativeVideoConcepts/Outputs/WarlineCapture_Generative_Cinematic_JobPlan.json` - provider-ready job plan generated by the runner.
- `GenerativeVideoConcepts/Outputs/WarlineCapture_Generative_Cinematic_QA_Report.md` - current dry-run QA report.

## Generator


## Alignment Rules

- Marketing claims must stay aligned with `Design/WarlineCapture_Economy_Reward_Design.md`.
- Store and starter-pack copy must stay aligned with `Design/Monetization/WarlineCapture_Monetization_Strategy.md`.
- Visual shots must come from, or be validated against, `Design/VisualLock` and `Design/VisualReferences/2DIsometricProduction`.
- Generated videos must pass QA before human validation.
