# Campaign — five large comic missions on the map

Latest owner direction: Campaign and Skirmish share the same two-stage navigation and map-based mission presentation.

1. Choose a chapter in Campaign A or a map in Skirmish A.
2. Select a large individual thumbnail mission card directly on the map.
3. Read its matching preview/purpose and use the shared primary command.

Campaign shows five missions per chapter; Skirmish shows six per objective region. Use the same Back button, panel backgrounds, steel borders, typography, card spacing, selected states, blue secondary and green primary commands. The prior Campaign tiny mission drawer is superseded.

![Campaign five mission cards on the map](campaign-five-comic-missions-on-map.png)

Each chapter choice and each mission has its own comic thumbnail. Native implementation must reuse the exact corresponding Campaign comic bindings from the main page, and preserve existing progression, availability and actual mission copy. Generated example statuses and briefing copy are concept fixtures, not evidence of runtime readiness. No new physical map or gameplay content is authorized.
